About this edition

This game uses the 87 chapter/story entries listed in the table of contents of the Purnabhadra 1199 CE recension as presented in the public-domain 1925 Arthur W. Ryder English edition.

The underlying Panchatantra is ancient, and the selected source edition states that it is the Purnabhadra recension, the longest of the 25 Indian recensions described there. The game's prose, decision prompts, scoring and interface are newly written.

The game deliberately does not call its Wisdom Profile an IQ score. It measures performance inside this game.
